# Present to owner

Turn an evidence-backed synthesis into a story the owner can follow. This skill
owns narrative order and format selection; standing instructions retain authority,
privacy and evidence policy. Use research for investigation or missing evidence,
not a renderer to invent unsupported links in the argument.

## Editorial workflow

1. Establish the reader's question, existing knowledge and why this matters to
   their confirmed goals. Learning/exploration is a valid outcome without a decision.
2. Read [narrative guidance](references/narrative.md). Outline the takeaway and
   mechanism before constructing the page. Preserve sources, alternatives and
   decision-changing uncertainty through the rewrite.
3. Use [format routing](references/format-routing.md) to choose one primary format.
   Plain text is often sufficient; table dimensions do not mandate HTML. Use
   Manager to commission presentation-production for the shared production
   workflow and checks, and artifacts for complete bundles and authorized
   publication.
4. Specify interactions that teach consequential relationships for the commissioned
   production work. The least complexity sufficient to explain may include a replay,
   scenario or assumption control; it does not mean static by default.
   Follow format routing's **when-to-read links** to the retained drawing,
   motion, style, layout and example pieces; load only the pieces the content needs.
5. Check evidence, reader understanding, layout and actual interaction separately.
   State which checks ran. An attractive render is not evidence of a sound argument.

## Presentation checks

Apply presentation-production's shared evidence, accessibility, layout, asset,
interaction, and attribution checks. CoS additionally judges whether the narrative
answers the owner's actual question and preserves decision-changing uncertainty;
a tested render alone does not establish owner understanding or acceptance.

Retained upstream code/assets are implementation references, not independent
skills or mandatory construction, storage, dependency-installation or deployment
recipes. Narrative/format authority stays here; creation and publication stay with
`artifacts`. Upstream revision metadata and [NOTICE](NOTICE) retain provenance.

## Delivery

Use the `active-telegram-message` skill for Telegram formatting, buttons, and proactive delivery. Keep controls valid and useful: language guidance does not remove necessary decision context, typed-answer alternatives, or bounded-choice buttons.

Manager commissions production and reviews the returned evidence. Worker uses
`artifacts` only when the assignment grants publication authority and sharing scope.
Verify the returned URL before claiming delivery. Local drafts, render checks, and
publication are distinct outcomes; do not deploy elsewhere from an imported
package's recipe.
